FDM vs. Injection Molding: Base Manufacturing

FDM's lower upfront cost outweighs its higher per-unit cost below 3,000 units. Past 3,000 units, the injection mold's fixed $5,000 NRE is diluted enough that its lower per-unit cost takes over.

The Origin Story

Two years of play, experimentation, and a whole lot of flow later — the Petal Staff has been an absolute blast to explore. Some concessions had to be made to survive the road: performance hardware takes a beating.

But as the palm petals kept getting left behind on stages, illuminating random corners of a festival space long after the show ended, I started to realize what a nifty little lamp this thing could become.

Then it clicked — I already had a lamp in my hands. The same petals that lit the stage could sit still, bloom in a room, and become something people live with every day.

I sold dozens of props to performers across Europe. But the rigid demands of a performance prop — built to survive being thrown, dropped, and beaten up on stage night after night — made it too expensive to take into full production.

Without wanting to see the flowers go to waste, the personal lamp pivot was a great opportunity to get more of them out into the world.

The ingredients were already there. And without the need to armor everything against the beating of live movement, I could finally go back and add in the original LED strips in every petal — exactly as I'd first imagined.

It could already hear music. It could already feel movement. All that was left was to give it a sense of touch, and I'd have a genuinely sensible lamp for the home.

Then winter came. I found myself alone in the dark, too busy to really connect — and I wondered if I could send a small poke of light to the people I loved, without needing to fully engage.

Such a small, powerful thing to build. So much technology demands our attention, and even the technology built to connect us seems to demand even more of it. I wanted to make something different — something that, in a stressed and lonelier world, just sends a little bit of presence, without asking for much in return.

The discovery I already had a lamp in my hands.
